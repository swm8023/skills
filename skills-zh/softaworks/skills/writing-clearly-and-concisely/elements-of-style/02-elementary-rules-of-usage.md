## II. Elementary Rules Of Usage

### Rule 1. Form the possessive singular of nouns by adding 's.

无论最后一个辅音是什么，都遵循这条规则。因此应写作：

Charles's friend

Burns's poems

the witch's malice

这是美国政府印刷局和牛津大学出版社采用的用法。

例外包括以 *-es* 和 *-is* 结尾的古代专名所有格、*Jesus'*，以及 *for conscience' sake*、*for righteousness' sake* 等形式。但 *Achilles' heel*、*Moses' laws*、*Isis' temple* 通常可改为：

the heel of Achilles

the laws of Moses

the temple of Isis

代词所有格 *hers*、*its*、*theirs*、*yours* 和 *oneself* 不加撇号。

### Rule 2. In a series of three or more terms with a single conjunction, use a comma after each term except the last.

因此应写作：

red, white, and blue

gold, silver, or copper

He opened the letter, read it, and made a note of its contents.

这也是美国政府印刷局和牛津大学出版社采用的用法。

公司名称中省略最后一个逗号，例如：

Brown, Shipley & Co.

### Rule 3. Enclose parenthetic expressions between commas.

The best way to see a country, unless you are pressed for time, is to travel on foot.

这条规则不易应用：单个词（如 *however*）或简短短语是否属于插入语，往往很难判断。对句子流畅性的打断很轻时，可以不加逗号。但无论打断程度轻重，都不能只加一边的逗号而省略另一边。以下标点用法：

Marjorie's husband, Colonel Nelson paid us a visit yesterday,

或

My brother you will be pleased to hear, is now in perfect health,

都站不住脚。

如果插入语前有连词，应把第一个逗号放在连词前，而非连词后。

He saw us coming, and unaware that we had learned of his treachery, greeted us with a smile.

以下内容必须视为插入语，并用一对逗号括起；若在句末，则前用逗号、后用句号：

\(1\) 日期中的年份，以及星期后面的月日：

February to July, 1916.

April 6, 1917.

Monday, November 11, 1918.

\(2\) 缩写 *etc.* 和 *jr.*

\(3\) 非限制性关系从句，即不用于辨认或界定先行名词的从句，以及由时间或地点连词引出的类似从句。

The audience, which had at first been indifferent, became more and more interested.

此句中 *which* 引出的从句没有用来区分究竟是哪一群观众；所指观众应已明确。这个从句以插入方式补充主句的信息。整个句子实际上结合了两个原本可以独立表达的陈述：

The audience had at first been indifferent. It became more and more interested.

对比下面不加逗号隔开的限制性关系从句：

The candidate who best meets these requirements will obtain the place.

这里由 *who* 引出的从句，确实用于辨认究竟指哪一位候选人；这个句子不能拆成两个独立陈述。

以下两句的标点差异，也基于相同原则：

Nether Stowey, where Coleridge wrote The Rime of the Ancient Mariner, is a few miles from Bridgewater.

The day will come when you will admit your mistake.

Nether Stowey 的名称已经明确指认该地，因此关于 Coleridge 的陈述属于补充性插入语。后一句中的 *day* 只能靠从句界定，因此从句具有限制性。

用逗号隔开句子主句前后的短语或从句，与用逗号括起插入语的原则相似。

Partly by hard fighting, partly by diplomatic skill, they enlarged their dominions to the east, and rose to royal rank with the possession of Sicily, exchanged afterwards for Sardinia.

其他例子可参见规则 4、5、6、7、16、18 下引用的句子。

作者应注意，不要用逗号隔开独立分句：见规则 5。

### Rule 4. Place a comma before a conjunction introducing a co-ordinate clause.

The early records of the city have disappeared, and the story of its first years can no longer be reconstructed.

The situation is perilous, but there is still one chance of escape.

这类句子脱离上下文时，似乎需要重写。因为到了逗号处，前半句的意思已经完整，后半句就像事后补充。此外，*and* 是最不具体的连接词：用于独立分句之间时，只表示二者存在联系，却不说明联系是什么。在上例中，关系是因果。两个句子可以改写为：

As the early records of the city have disappeared, the story of its first years can no longer be reconstructed.

Although the situation is perilous, there is still one chance of escape.

也可以用短语替代从句：

Owing to the disappearance of the early records of the city, the story of its first years can no longer be reconstructed.

In this perilous situation, there is still one chance of escape.

但如果句子一律紧凑、掉尾，作者也可能走向另一极端。偶尔使用松散句，可以避免文体过分正式，让读者稍作放松。因此，前述松散句在轻松自然的文字中很常见。不过不应反复照这种模式造句（见规则 14）。

两部分句子中，如果第二部分由 *as*（意为 *because*）、*for*、*or*、*nor*、*while*（意为 *and at the same time*）引出，也应在连词前加逗号。

如果第二部分由副词引出，应使用分号而非逗号（见规则 5）。*so* 和 *yet* 可以作副词，也可以作连词，取决于作者将第二分句视为并列还是从属，因此两种标点都有可能合理。但 *so* 的这些用法（相当于 *accordingly* 或 *so that*）较为口语化，书面语中通常应避免。一个常用的简单改法，是去掉 *so*，用 *as* 或 *since* 开始第一分句：

| 原句 | 改写 |
| --- | --- |
| I had never been in the place before; so I had difficulty in finding my way about. | As I had never been in the place before, I had difficulty in finding my way about. |

如果第二独立分句之前有应以逗号隔开的从句或引导短语，则连词后不需要再加逗号。

The situation is perilous, but if we are prepared to act promptly, there is still one chance of escape.

如果两分句主语相同且只出现一次，连词为 *but* 时需要逗号；连词为 *and*，且两个陈述关系紧密或直接时，应省略逗号。

I have heard his arguments, but am still unconvinced.

He has had several years' experience and is thoroughly competent.

### Rule 5. Do not join independent clauses by a comma.

两个或多个语法完整、没有连词连接的分句组成一个复合句时，应使用分号。

Stevenson's romances are entertaining; they are full of exciting adventures.

It is nearly half past five; we cannot reach town before dark.

当然，上面的例子也可以将分号改为句号，分别写成两个句子。

Stevenson's romances are entertaining. They are full of exciting adventures.

It is nearly half past five. We cannot reach town before dark.

如果加上连词，适当的标点就是逗号（规则 4）。

Stevenson's romances are entertaining, for they are full of exciting adventures.

It is nearly half past five, and we cannot reach town before dark.

比较以上三种形式，就能看出第一种的优点。至少在这些例子中，它优于第二种，因为它明确暗示两个陈述关系紧密，而第二种没有这样做；也优于第三种，因为更简洁、更有力。事实上，用这种简单方法表达陈述之间的联系，是行文最有用的技巧之一。如上所示，这种联系通常是原因或结果。

注意，第二分句前如果是 *accordingly*、*besides*、*then*、*therefore*、*thus* 等副词，而非连词，仍需使用分号。

这条规则可以有两个例外。分句很短、形式相似时，通常允许使用逗号：

Man proposes, God disposes.

The gate swung apart, the bridge fell, the portcullis was drawn up.

注意，这些例子中没有因果关系。另外，以下口语表达：

I hardly knew him, he was so changed,

必须用逗号而非分号。但除故事或戏剧中的对话，或熟人书信之外，这种表达不适合书面文字。

### Rule 6. Do not break sentences in two.

换句话说，不要把应使用逗号的地方写成句号。

I met them on a Cunard liner several years ago. Coming home from Liverpool to New York.

He was an interesting talker. A man who had traveled all over the world and lived in half a dozen countries.

以上两个例子中的第一个句号都应改为逗号，后面的单词首字母改为小写。

可以让强调的词或短语独立成句，并据此使用标点：

Again and again he called out. No reply.

但作者必须确认，这种强调有必要，且读者不会误以为是语法或标点失误。

规则 3、4、5、6 涵盖一般句子最重要的标点原则，应熟练掌握，使应用成为自然习惯。

### Rule 7. A participial phrase at the beginning of a sentence must refer to the grammatical subject.

Walking slowly down the road, he saw a woman accompanied by two children.

*walking* 修饰句子的主语，而非那位女士。如果要让它修饰女士，就必须改写句子：

He saw a woman accompanied by two children, walking slowly down the road.

句首由连词或介词引出的分词短语、同位语名词、形容词和形容词短语，也遵循同一规则。

| 原句 | 改写 |
| --- | --- |
| On arriving in Chicago, his friends met him at the station. | When he arrived (or, On his arrival) in Chicago, his friends met him at the station. |
| A soldier of proved valor, they entrusted him with the defence of the city. | A soldier of proved valor, he was entrusted with the defence of the city. |
| Young and inexperienced, the task seemed easy to me. | Young and inexperienced, I thought the task easy. |
| Without a friend to counsel him, the temptation proved irresistible. | Without a friend to counsel him, he found the temptation irresistible. |

违反这条规则的句子，常常会显得可笑。

Being in a dilapidated condition, I was able to buy the house very cheap.

Wondering irresolutely what to do next, the clock struck twelve.
